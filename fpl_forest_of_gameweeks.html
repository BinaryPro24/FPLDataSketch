How to read the forest
Trunk height = points earned · width = transfers made
Canopy colour = mood that week (red → amber → green)
Fallen fruit = bench points wasted
Glow at roots = net transfer points (green gain / red loss)
Gold halo = chip played that week
River = overall rank, flowing left to right
▲▼ above canopy = rank moved up / down that week
